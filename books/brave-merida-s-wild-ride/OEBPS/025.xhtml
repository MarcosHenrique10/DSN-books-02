The stallion stood quietly. Merida looked in his
 eyes for an answer to what had caused the wild ride.
 Something sparked her memory and she removed the
 bridle. His head moved softly as if he were nodding,
 before he galloped down the misty shoreline.

Merida frowned as she watched him. Was he really
 racing into the water, or was it the fog playing tricks on
 her eyesight?
