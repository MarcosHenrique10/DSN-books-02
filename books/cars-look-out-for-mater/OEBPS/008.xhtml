One day, Sally and Lightning drove to a
 cliff overlooking a gravel pit. “That seems like
 a fun place to race,” said Lightning. “I’ll have
 to bring Mater there.”

“Please don’t,” Sally begged. “I’m always
 warning Mater that this place is dangerous.
 I don’t want to worry while I’m out of town
 the next few days. Promise me you’ll look
 out for Mater while I’m away.”
