Luckily, Mater wasn’t hurt. But he was
 stuck. “I can get you down,” Big Bull fumed.
 “But I’ll have to break your tow cable to do it.”

Once Mater was free, he was as quiet as
 could be. What kind of tow truck was he
 without his cable?

Lightning felt bad, too. He should have
 done a better job looking out for Mater.
