But no one was more excited about Easter than Mater the
 tow truck. “I can’t wait for the Easter Buggy!”
 he told Lightning.

Lightning just smiled. He didn’t believe in the Easter Buggy.

“I’m going to stay up all night,” Mater said.

“No one’s ever seen the Easter Buggy,” Lightning reminded him.

“I know it,” Mater said. “That’s why I
 have a plan.”
