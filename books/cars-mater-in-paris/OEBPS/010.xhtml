In Paris, Mater and Lightning met up with Holley and Finn.

“We’ve been tailing these Lemons for awhile now, but they
 keep giving us the slip,” said Finn.

“Them Lemons are tricky,” said Mater. “You just
gotta learn how to think like them.”

“What would you do if you were a Lemon?” asked Holley.

“Well, that’s easy. I’d make sure I had plenty of spare parts,”
 said Mater.

“Brilliant. You and Lightning can visit Tomber,
 the spare parts dealer, at the marketplace. Holley and I
 will head to the markets on the West side of Paris.”
