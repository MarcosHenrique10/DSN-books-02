The Hugos charged
 forward. They tried to ram
 Lightning from the side,
 but he was too quick and
 caused them to crash into
 each other.

Two more sprayed
 knockout gas. But Mater
 spun his tow hook! It blew
 the gas back at the Hugos.
 The two thugs instantly
 passed out.

“We’d better split up, buddy!” Mater told Lightning.
 “You go that way, and I’ll go this way.”
